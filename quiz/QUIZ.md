
The intelligence of an individual:

  - can be measured and quantified reliably
  - is a socially defined phenomenon, which does not exist within an artifact


Artificial Intelligence is defined as:

  - an intelligent service, such as a chat bot provided by OpenAI, Anthropic, Perplexity, etc.
  - a benchmark standard, determined by an advisory board from Google, Microsoft, and Meta
  - a practice, where people and machines collaborate to enhance capabilities

  - the emergence of superintelligence
  - artificial general intelligence (AGI)
  - the financial bubble caused by "AI Hype" deception bursts


It is grammatically correct to:

  - "according to the AIs"
  - "the AI was thinking, then it said something"
  - none of the above

AI is a practice, not an individual, therefore the term has no pronouns or articles in usage.
